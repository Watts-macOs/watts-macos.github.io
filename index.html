How It Works

Step 1: Install and Launch

Drag Watts to your Applications folder and open from the menu bar. It integrates instantly, mirroring macOS battery display with enhanced details.

Step 2: Monitor and Assess

View real-time stats: capacity, cycles, health. Receive notifications on status changes and calibration needs.

Step 3: Calibrate with Guidance

Follow the assistant: charge to 100%, discharge fully, recharge. Schedule or postpone as required; history logs every session.

Step 4: Track Progress

Review logged data over time. Adjust settings for vertical icon or custom scheduling—precision maintained effortlessly.
